Annual dragon art calendar e-shop for the Draci.info community. The calendar tradition started in 2008, originally organised by community member Dragarta. Vojtěch built the e-shop website from scratch when it went public in 2016, and later took over managing the project entirely. Features artwork from Czech and Slovak dragon artists, available in Czech and English with worldwide shipping.
