Open-source 3D printed construction set with large parts that snap together without screws, printable without supports. Includes gears for mechanical builds — developed originally as an alternative to Lego/Duplo for his kids, allowing them to build mechanical contraptions. Designed in OpenSCAD. Presented at Maker Faire Olomouc 2024 and featured in the Maker Faire Yearbook 2025.
